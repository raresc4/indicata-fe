1. Presentation of the state of facts Constatarea stării de fapt

Universitatea Politehnica Timișoara carries out its doctoral education through the Council for Doctoral University Studies (CSUD) and accredited doctoral schools. The University Charter, art. 15, establishes the Senate and the Board of Administration as the deliberative and executive governance organs responsible for strategic resource allocation . The doctoral school in Computer Engineering operates under direct internal oversight, with executive responsibilities delegated to the Doctoral School Council .

All administrative departments submit bi-annual operational status reports directly to the Vice-Rector for Academic Quality
